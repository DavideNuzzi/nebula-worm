How it works

  1. Transition. The model's state is a point in a 3-D latent space. Every 0.34 s (one frame of the recording) the transition network draws the next point.
  2. Decoders. Two recurrent decoders read the latent trajectory and output the activity of 109 neurons and the behavior.
  3. Closed loop. In closed-loop mode the decoded activity, with the silenced neurons held low, is encoded back into the latent space before the next transition. In generative mode the transition runs on its own.
  4. Body. Each behavior sets a body wave (eigenworm phase and turn amplitude). The resulting curvature drives a Newtonian model of the worm's body on agar (ElegansBot), integrated every 50 µs.

Model: NEBULA, trained on the whole-brain recording of worm 1 of Kato et al., Global brain dynamics embed the motor command sequence of Caenorhabditis elegans, Cell 163, 656–669 (2015).
